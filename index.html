Overview

This tool performs a comprehensive algorithmic trading backtest for Bitcoin (BTC) using approximately 2.7 years (1,000 days) of real historical price data. It simulates how a specific technical strategy would have performed compared to a simple "buy and hold" approach.

Data Source

Price data is fetched live from the Binance API (api.binance.com). The simulation retrieves the daily OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) data for the BTC/USDT pair. Timestamps are converted from Unix milliseconds to ISO date strings for processing.

Technical Indicators

  • SMA7: Fast Simple Moving Average (7-day window). Used to identify short-term trend direction.
  • SMA30: Slow Simple Moving Average (30-day window). Used to identify long-term trend momentum.
  • RSI-14: Relative Strength Index (14-day window). A momentum oscillator that measures the speed and change of price movements on a scale of 0 to 100. Overbought > 70, Oversold < 30.
  • Bollinger Bands: A volatility envelope consisting of a 20-day SMA and two outer bands plotted at ±2 standard deviations from the middle SMA.

Trading Algorithm

The strategy employs a Golden Cross / Death Cross mechanism with an RSI confirmation filter:

  • BUY Signal: Triggered when the SMA7 crosses above the SMA30 (Golden Cross) AND the RSI is below 70 (ensuring the asset is not overbought).
  • SELL Signal: Triggered when the SMA7 crosses below the SMA30 (Death Cross) AND the RSI is above 30 (ensuring the asset is not oversold).
  • Position Sizing: The simulation starts with $10,000 USD and utilizes 100% of available capital for each trade (all-in/all-out).

Performance Metrics

  • Final Portfolio Value: The ending total value of cash and BTC holdings.
  • Total Return %: Percentage gain or loss of the strategy from inception.
  • Buy & Hold Return %: Passive benchmark performance if you had simply bought $10,000 of BTC on Day 1.
  • Alpha: The strategy's outperformance (or underperformance) relative to the Buy & Hold benchmark.
  • Total Trades: The number of completed buy-to-sell round trips.
  • Win Rate %: Percentage of completed trades that resulted in a profit.
  • Max Drawdown %: The largest peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value, representing historical risk.
  • Sharpe Ratio: A measure of risk-adjusted return, calculated using annualized daily returns and their standard deviation (assumes 365-day trading).

Chart Guide

Price Chart: The main window displays the BTC closing price (white), SMA7 (orange), SMA30 (blue), and Bollinger Bands (shaded region). Green triangles indicate BUY signals; red triangles indicate SELL signals.

Portfolio Chart: Displays the growth of the Algo Strategy (solid neon green) versus the Buy & Hold benchmark (dashed gray line).
